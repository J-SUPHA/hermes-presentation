What you can do with Hermes

The agent that grows with you

Morning briefing on your phone "Every weekday at 7am, check my calendar and email, summarize the day's meetings, and text it to my Telegram before I wake up."
Watches things for you "Check flight prices to Tokyo every 6 hours and ping me the moment they drop below $800." Works for product prices, GitHub issues, competitor news — anything.
Meeting notes → action items Paste raw meeting notes, get a clean list of decisions, owners, and deadlines — and have it file the tickets in Linear or GitHub Issues itself.
Researches while you sleep "Read these 5 papers and have a comparison table ready by morning." It searches, reads, takes notes in its own memory, and builds on them next session.
Lives in your group chat Add it to a Discord or Slack channel — it answers questions with full context of your docs, and remembers what the team decided last month.
Automates the boring parts "Rename these 200 photos by date, organize my Downloads folder, and back it all up to the cloud every Sunday night." Done, and scheduled forever.
